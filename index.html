Heavy Day 50 min · 6:30–7:20

  1. Step in, step out + mobility 5 min
  2. Power clean — build to top 4×__
  3. Trap bar deadlift 4×__
  4. Chin-up or pull 4×__
  5. Bench press or push 4×__
  6. Hip strength 3 sets
  7. Ab strength 3 sets
  8. Calves — a lot 4 sets
  9. Posterior delts 3 sets
First set is the warm-up. Always.

Form Day 50 min · trims day-before-game

  1. Step in, step out + pogo hops 3 min
  2. Power clean — speed, crisp 3×__
  3. Jump — CMJ or low box 3 sets
  4. Squat or trap bar — light, fast 3×__
  5. Hip strength 2 sets
  6. Ab strength 2 sets
  7. Calves 3 sets
  8. Posterior delts 2 sets
Day before a game: bar work + jumps only, out in 20.

Without question, you can get stronger during the season, and this is designed for making every workout count. Everyone on this team is at a point where showing up on lift days is not a question — the commitment is clearly already there. Heavy days are tight sessions focused on strength and power development. Form days are neurologically focused — form and recovery movements that keep the system sharp without adding fatigue. Fatigue is a function of volume. Adaptation is a function of intensity. Two well-run sessions, both real, both purposeful, will move the needle all season long.

Critical assumptions — recovery is not optional

  • Sleep. Lights out by 8 PM, phone away, fall asleep in short order. Pack your bag the night before — a clean morning starts the night before.
  • Wake up right. Get light in your eyes early. It resets the clock for the next night.
  • Eat and drink water. All day, not just around lifts.
  • Creatine. Start at 5 g, mixed into your shake.
  • Post-workout shake, every time. 25–35 g protein — about a scoop and a half — plus berries for a little glycogen back. Adjust up as it feels right and your stomach agrees.

Block C — Basketball In-Season · Nov 30 – Feb 5 · Two Draft Options

Heavy days want ~72 hours before tip-off — major muscle groups, compound lifts. Rapid recovery days flush and prime. Day after a game? We can lift.

Non-basketball girls lift full volume on every heavy day — they aren't game-gated. The trim notes on this calendar apply to basketball players only. ◎ = circled on the schedule.

🏀 Basketballnothing from us that day

Heavy Day 50 min · wants 72h before a game

The honest strength stimulus — major muscle groups, compound movements: Bulgarian split squats, squats, heavy cleans, heavy hex bar lifts, plus a high volume of push and pull — bench press and pull-ups. This is the day that needs the two-plus nights of sleep on the far side; that's why every heavy on this calendar is placed against the game schedule first.

Fatigue comes from volume — gains come from intensity.

Rapid Recovery full 50 min

A real session, not a day off — the same 50 minutes, pointed the other direction. Smaller muscle groups, the important stuff: hips, core, calves, posterior delts, mobility, and low-level jump work to flush soreness and keep the spring. The goal is simple — recover faster between games. When it lands the day before a game, it's the primer: same 50 minutes, same purpose.

Recover faster. That's the whole job.
